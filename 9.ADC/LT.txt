- ADC: Analog Digital Convert 12-bit
- 0 - 3.3V -> 0 - 4095
- 0 - 1    -> 0 - 4095

- ADC loai 12-bit: 0 - 4095
- Dien ap dau vao: 0 - 3.3V
- Co the tu gia tri ADC dau vao quy ra dien ap dau vao

- Thoi gian chuyen doi (Dien ap -> ADC)
t_CONV = Thoi gian lay mau + 12.5 chu ky
- Ve qua trinh chuyen doi lay mau: Thoi gian lay mau
+ Cang ngan: Thi nhanh nhung de nhieu
+ Cang nhieu: Thi cham nhung se dung / chac chan hon

- Cac che do hoat dong cua ADC
+ Chuyen doi don: Do 1 lan roi xong
+ Chuyen doi lien tuc: Do lien tuc nhieu lan
=> Chi su dung cho 1 kenh
+ Che do quet: Cho phep do ADC nhieu kenh (Toi da 10 kenh PA0 - PB1)

+ Analog Watchdog: Flag dc set len 1 neu KQ chuyen doi < hoac > nguong set
+ Dual mode: Su dung 2 bo ADC de do song song
	-> ADC co 3 bo ADC1, ADC2, ADC3 va do uu tien thap dan (ADC1 > ADC2 > ADC3)
	-> Dual mode: La su dung 2 bo ADC cho qua trinh chuyen doi
	
- Phan biet 2 nhom kenh: Regular va Injected
+ Regular: Thong dung nhat, hay su dung nhat: 10 kenh
-> Hang doi chuan - Bo chuyen doi chuan
-> Tac dung: Do adc, do cac cam bien
-> Dung chung 1 thanh ghi ket qua 16-bit ten la ADC_DR  [11 : 0]
-> Nguy co de du lieu cao, no de bi ngat (NVIC) neu 1 kenh ADC co muu uu tien cao hon

+ Injected: Bo cap cao (VIP)
-> Tac dung: Doc tin hieu khan cap theo thoi gian thuc (Real-time)
-> Chi co 4 kenh / chu ky
-> No co 4 thanh ghi rieng cho tung bo Injected (ADC_JDRx)
-> Khong bi ghi de, no co the ngat ngang 1 bo Regular

- Sau khi chuyen doi xong, 1 co (Flag) duoc dung len ten la
EOC: End of Convert
Ko dung NVIC de auto doc, ma no dung DMA
